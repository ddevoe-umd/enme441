


stepper.py
    Drive 1 motor directly from a ULN2803 Darlington array

stepper_with_shifter.py
    Drive 1 motor through a shift register + ULN2803

stepper_thread.py
    Drive 2 motors directly from a ULN2803
    using threads for concurrency (with major parralelism limitations)

stepper_async.py
    Drive 2 motors directly from a ULN2803
    using asyncio for concurrency (with speed limitations)

stepper_async_timer.py
    Drive 2 motors directly from a ULN2803
    using asyncio + hardware timer for concurrency at high speed

stepper_async_timer_shifter.py
    Drive 2 motors through a shift register + ULN2803
    using asyncio + hardware timer for concurrency at high speed
